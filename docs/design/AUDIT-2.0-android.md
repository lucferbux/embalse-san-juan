# Audit 2.0: Android (phone, unfolded foldable, Glance widgets)

Adversarial review of the Android rebuild on the 2.0 design (docs/design/review-2.0/SYNTHESIS.md incl. "Owner decisions",
lead-5-android.md, CLAUDE.md "Android"), from the captures in `build/ux-review/android-2.0/` (8 Nov 2026, 18:39–18:47)
and the code in `android/app/src/main/java/com/lucferbux/embalsesanjuan/`. iOS reference: `build/ux-review/iphone-69/`.
Paths below are relative to that package unless they start with `android/` or `build/`.

## Verdict

**Not shippable.** The captured default-size screens mostly keep the card's rules, but the rule the owner made the
centre of 2.0, *the figure wholly in the water or wholly in the sky*, is broken by the code in two places the captures
happen not to show: the "Ahora" widget at its default 2×2 size (110 dp) puts the lower part of the figure block inside
the water for any level above about 13 %, and the card's year view, when it sends the block to the sky, places it above
the *lowest* point of the surface instead of the highest, so on a short card (every reservoir page) the dark figure
lands on the water. The single worst problem is the widget: it is the surface people see most, and at the size the
picker drops it in it draws today's 46,6 % across the waterline. Close behind: the Histórico chart has no axes at all
and its legend's solid grey "Media" swatch matches only an unlabelled 50 % gridline, so the average is misread; the
year view's "hoy" label straddles the crest; the river, the river module and the small widget give no source or date.

**Counts:** 3 Blocker, 16 Major, 23 Minor, 2 Idea.

**Coverage.** Opened every image in `android-2.0/` (10) and, for parity, `iphone-69/light-01-ahora.png` and
`large-01-ahora.png`. Not captured, so **unreviewed** (risk, not proven clean): dark and large for year, Histórico and El
río; the reservoir pages (Burguillo, Picadas) in either mode; the module detail screens, Ficha and Sugerencias; the
collapsed water strip; a tablet or desktop window; a phone in landscape; the English locale; Android 11 and earlier
(static water-blue scheme); widgets in dark mode, stale, at other sizes or at a larger font scale. The foldable captures
were made with `wm size` on a phone AVD (see `build/ux-review/MANIFEST.txt`), so there is **no hinge** in them:
fold postures and the hinge rule are unverified. Motion (the wave, the 900 ms Hoy↔Año morph, pull-to-refresh) and
TalkBack cannot be judged from stills; findings about them come from the code and say so.

## Keep

- One figure-placement function shared by the card's two modes (fix its year sky case, don't fork it).
- The fixed water hue against dynamic-colour chrome: the card reads as the same object on every wallpaper (`now.png`, `dark-now.png`).
- Source footers on every module tile and the "Cada línea es el dato semanal de MITECO…" notes.
- SAIH and MITECO kept in separate panels everywhere captured.
- `NavigationSuiteScaffold` switching bar ↔ rail by window size (`now.png` vs `fold-now.png`).
- `Format.kt`'s Spanish number style (`46,6 %`, `64,1 hm³`) with the thin space.
