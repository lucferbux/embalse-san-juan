# Audit 2.0: Apple (iPhone, iPad, iOS widgets)

Adversarial review of the 2.0 redesign (docs/design/review-2.0/SYNTHESIS.md, "Owner decisions", and CLAUDE.md "Layout"),
from the capture of 8 Nov 2026 16:32Z (`c758b1e`) in `build/ux-review/`. Widget snapshots render in the test host's
English locale ("Steady", `48.7 %`) and the fixture snapshot is older than the history fixture; neither is reported.

## Verdict

**Not shippable.** In default text sizes the redesign mostly holds its own rules, but at the largest accessibility sizes
the water card breaks in every format: the figure block is clipped at the right edge (the percentage, the capacity, the
trend and the source date are cut), it rides up over the "Hoy | Año" switch, and in the year view the switch is pushed out
of the card altogether, so a VoiceOver/Dynamic Type user who taps "Año" cannot get back to today, and today's point on the
surface is clipped off. The single worst problem is that clipping: it makes the one answer the app exists to give
(`46,6 %`, how much, since when) unreadable for exactly the users the Dynamic Type rule protects. Close behind: the medium
widget puts a MITECO figure in the SAIH panel, the stale small widget draws the figure over its own warning, and the year
view's "hoy" label is printed in black straddling the waterline, the one thing the new rule forbids.

**Counts:** 6 Blocker, 15 Major, 27 Minor, 5 Idea.

**Coverage.** Opened: every `iphone-69` light screen, the iPhone dark and large screens for 01 and 09, compact light 01, 05,
09, compact large 01, 02, 03, 04, 09, iPad portrait light 01, 04, 09 and large 01, and 26 of 27 widget images. Not opened
(risk, not proven clean): the remaining dark/large iPhone screens 02–08, compact light/dark 02–04, 06–08, iPad portrait 02,
03, 05–08 in all modes, iPad dark, and the widget `dark-medium.png`. `ipad-landscape/` is from 7 Nov with the old screen names, `duo-outer/` is empty and `duo-inner` was
never captured: the regular-wide pane, the sidebar and the Duo fold rule are **unreviewed** (see A-M14).

- Figure wholly in the water in white at 46,6 % on the 6.9" and iPad (`iphone-69/light-01-ahora.png`): the waterline never cuts the block in default sizes.
- Dark sky and water in dark mode (`iphone-69/dark-01-ahora.png`, `widgets-ios/dark-small.png`): no navy-on-black mush.
- One-source modules with source and date in the footer (`iphone-69/light-01-ahora.png`).
- El río's three tanks with the selected one outlined (`iphone-69/light-03-el-rio.png`).
- Widget extremes 8/30/62/97 % switch cleanly between sky and water (`widgets-ios/light-small-*.png`).
