# Embalse San Juan 1.0: adversarial UX audit, Android formats and iPad landscape

Reviewer: `ux-adversary`. Second pass. Input: `build/ux-review/` from the run of 2026-11-07T11:56Z (commit `cf17a25` with uncommitted changes to `scripts/capture-matrix.sh` and `scripts/lib/android-emu.sh`) for `android-phone`, `android-foldable` and `android-tablet`, and the run of 2026-11-07T11:16Z (commit `e3644bf`) for `ipad-landscape`.
Formats: `android-phone` (1080×2400 @ 420, 411 × 914 dp), `android-foldable` (2208×1840 @ 420, 841 × 701 dp, an open book-style foldable emulated by size only), `android-tablet` (2560×1600 @ 320, 1280 × 800 dp), each in light, dark and large (font scale 2.0); `ipad-landscape` in light, dark and large. All 54 Android images were opened as 900 px copies, and the heroes of `android-phone/light-01`, `large-01`, `android-foldable/light-01`, `large-01`, `android-tablet/light-01` and `large-01` were also cropped at full resolution. The 24 iPad landscape images were reviewed in the first pass and have not changed.
Code read: `android/app/src/main/java/com/lucferbux/embalsesanjuan/**` (`ui/ReservoirScreen.kt`, `ui/Components.kt`, `ui/SecondaryScreens.kt`, `ui/Theme.kt`, `widget/ReservoirWidget.kt`, `data/Format.kt`, `MainActivity.kt`), `res/xml/reservoir_widget_info.xml`, `scripts/capture-matrix.sh`, `scripts/lib/android-emu.sh`, `ios/App/ContentView.swift`, `ios/App/Dashboard.swift`. The Android app code has not changed since the first pass; only the capture did.
Rules judged against: `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/SPEC-v1.md`, Material 3 and Material 3 Expressive, window size classes (compact < 600 dp, medium 600–839 dp, expanded ≥ 840 dp, large ≥ 1200 dp), Jetpack WindowManager fold postures, Material You dynamic colour, Android accessibility (TalkBack, font scale up to 2.0 with non-linear scaling, Remove animations), Google Play large-screen quality guidelines, Glance. Apple findings are in [AUDIT-1.0-apple.md](AUDIT-1.0-apple.md) and are not repeated here. Section O maps each one to Android. `docs/design/DESIGN-2.0.md` does not exist yet.
Known capture artefact, ignored: demo mode repeats the wifi icon in the status bar (two on the phone, three to four on the foldable, six to seven on the tablet).

## Verdict

1.0 is **not shippable to production on Google Play**. The first pass was made on a broken capture. This one shows the real app in every format and mode, and it confirms the worst problems. The single worst problem is still the hero. `WaterHero` draws its text twice, and each copy is clipped by the animated wave, so the waterline cuts *through the glyphs* of the one number. Where it happens:
- **At the default text size:** on the foldable, the tops of "8" and "%" are navy and the rest is white (`android-foldable/light-01-ahora.png`).
- **At font scale 2.0, in every format:** the tablet's "46,8 %" is cut almost in half (`android-tablet/large-01-ahora.png`). On the phone, the volume line breaks across the waterline: "64,46 hm³ de 138" is navy in the sky and "hm³" white in the water (`android-phone/large-01-ahora.png`).
- **Everywhere else, only by luck:** the phone and the tablet in light mode clear the crest by 4 to 6 dp, which is a matter of where the wave was when the shot was taken.

The new captures add three findings at font scale 2.0, alongside the three the first pass already had (letter-stacked rows, the lost chip, cut chart labels):
- `ValueRow` breaks words with no hyphen in four screens, not one: "Mínim / o", "Máx / imo", "Agua embalsa / da", "M / u / n / i / c / i / p / i / o".
- The history range control reads "Desde" instead of "Desde 1988".
- The hero text block climbs to 8 dp from the top of the card.

The tablet, seen for the first time:
- It doesn't crash.
- It is a phone layout spread over 1280 dp: two 610 dp columns with no maximum width, a 280 dp hero strip holding a 64 sp number, and secondary screens 1250 dp wide.
- Its staggered grid moves sections between columns when the text size changes, so the order isn't even stable for one user.

Material You still paints the water lavender in dark mode in all three formats. The "Caudal desembalsado" block is the only data block with no source and no time, on Android and on iOS. What is still unreviewed: fold postures, the Glance widget, the 1988 history and Wear OS.

## Implications for 2.0

These build on the Apple audit's 2.0 direction: full-screen water at the real level, the three sections Ahora, Este año and El río, a fill-level palette, and one number style everywhere. Each requirement cites the findings it comes from.

### Android phone (compact)

1. **Text never straddles the waterline, and there is no double-clip trick.** One text block in one colour, placed wholly in the water or wholly above the highest crest with 12 dp of clearance, whatever the wave phase. Verified at 8, 30, 47, 62 and 97 %, at font scale 1.0 and 2.0 (H2, H7, A1).
2. **The first viewport answers the question at 411 dp, at font scale 1.0 and 2.0.** The percentage, "64,5 hm³ de 138 hm³", the trend as a word plus an amount, and the source and time are all visible without scrolling. The hero grows with its text and never clips (H7, H11, I1).
3. **Every label survives font scale 2.0.** Rows stack label over value instead of breaking words. Chips flow. Segmented labels never truncate. Chart labels are measured. No fixed-width text slot anywhere (H3, H4, H5, H6, H23).
4. **Every block carries its source and time,** including outflow, and SAIH and MITECO are never adjacent without a label (H12, A6).
5. **The water has its own colour; Material You owns the chrome.** A harmonised water hue, tested with four wallpaper seeds, the pre-Android-12 fallback, and medium and high system contrast. Text in the water is white and at least 4.5:1 in every state (H8).
6. **One number style, shared with iOS and the web,** checked by the same fixture test in Kotlin: precision table, no-break spaces, real minus, units on every axis (A2, A5, H20).
7. **Motion is optional.** The water's motion (2.0 adds device tilt) stops with Remove animations and after a few seconds idle, and has a static fallback that is just as readable (H18).
8. **TalkBack reads one sentence per figure, in Spanish, with its source and time.** Charts have a text alternative, and bars are not progress indicators (H13, H14).
9. **M3 Expressive where it helps the glance:** a flexible top app bar with a subtitle, a connected button group for ranges, `LoadingIndicator` in loading states, and expressive shape on the water surface. No decorative motion beyond the water (H28).
10. **Deterministic captures of every state gate the release:** a frozen wave phase, the same scroll target in every mode, the 1988 range, and the offline, stale, error and loading states, plus a fast, calm start with no main-thread I/O (H1, H16, H18, H19).

### Android foldable

1. **Layout comes from `WindowInfoTracker` and `FoldingFeature`, never from symmetry.** The hinge's bounds set the column gap, as `FoldGeometry` does on iOS (J2).
2. **Book posture (half-opened, vertical hinge):** Ahora fills the left pane and Este año or El río the right one. Nothing crosses the hinge (J1, J3).
3. **Tabletop posture (horizontal hinge):** the water and the number in the top half, the scrolling sections and controls in the bottom half (J1).
4. **Open flat, portrait (about 700 dp):** a medium width gets two columns or one 600 dp column, never a stretched phone list (J5, H9).
5. **No control, number, label or chart annotation on the hinge.** The range control and every chart live inside one pane, and the range control is never narrowed to fit a guess (J3, J4, H6).
6. **The water is continuous across the fold:** one waterline at the same height in both panes, so it reads as one reservoir.
7. **Fold and unfold keep state:** scroll position, selected range and draft suggestion survive the switch between outer and inner display (`rememberSaveable`; `configChanges` already avoids a restart).
8. **Secondary screens use pane scaffolds** (`ListDetailPaneScaffold`, `SupportingPaneScaffold`) on a separating fold (J4, K3).
9. **The outer display is the phone design,** and is captured as its own format.
10. **Captured on a real foldable AVD in every posture** (closed, flat, book, tabletop) in light, dark and large, before any 2.0 layout is approved (J1).

### Android tablet

1. **The water fills the window and Ahora is at least 40 % of the height,** with the percentage around 112 sp or more. The 24 h curve shares that surface, and the columns start below it (K2, H10, D2).
2. **The same section placement as iPad,** fixed and independent of content height and font scale, with SAIH and MITECO never side by side at the top (H10).
3. **Expanded widths cap the content at about 1200 dp, and large widths use three columns** (Ahora, Este año, El río). The title shares the content margin, and rows inside cards are no wider than 480 dp (K1, N1).
4. **Canonical layouts:** list-detail for history, ficha and suggestions, and a supporting pane for chart plus statistics (K3).
5. **History charts take at least 50 % of the height,** with the statistics beside them and monthly ticks along a wide axis (K3, H21, D4).
6. **Split screen and desktop windowing work at 420, 640 and 840 dp,** always chosen by size class, and are captured, as is portrait (K4, H9).
7. **Keyboard, mouse and stylus:** a refresh button and Ctrl+R, focus indicators, hover states, and Esc for back (H15).
8. **Readable widths:** forms and lists of figures at no more than 600 to 840 dp, with the send action next to the field (K3, J4, H29).
9. **A compact top bar on short windows,** so the first 150 dp are water, not an empty bar (H11).
10. **Meet Play's large-screen quality tier 2:** landscape and portrait, multi-window, keyboard navigation, state kept across resizes.

### Glance widgets (Android)

1. **Snapshot-tested before anything else:** every size, levels 8 to 97 % with matching volumes, stale, offline, no data, font scale 1.0 and 2.0, four dynamic seeds, rendered by `make widgets` (L1).
2. **One full-bleed surface with white text, as on iOS,** in a water hue that is harmonised and not taken from the wallpaper (CLAUDE.md, H8).
3. **The trend is a word on medium and larger, and arrow plus delta on small** (L2, G2).
4. **MITECO figures are labelled "MITECO" with their week,** grouped apart from SAIH, and named as in the app (L2, G3).
5. **The curve agrees with the water** (0 to capacity domain), and carries its period and its first and last values in hm³ (L2, G4, G5).
6. **The percentage never ellipsizes at any font scale or size** (L3).
7. **The system corner radius and `appWidgetBackground`,** with rounded bitmaps below API 31 (L4).
8. **Generated, live previews in the picker** (`providePreview`, `previewLayout`), and no false `reconfigurable` (L5).
9. **TalkBack reads one summary sentence** with source and time (L6).
10. **More sizes:** a 4 × 1 strip and a 4 × 2 river widget, exact-size bitmaps, and refresh only on network with an offline cue (L7, L8, L9).

### Wear OS (from first principles)

1. **The tile is the primary surface:** water filling the round tile at the real level, "46,8 %" centred, "Bajando · −0,4 hm³" and "SAIH · 11:00" under it, built with ProtoLayout Material 3 Expressive. Tapping opens the app (M2).
2. **Complications for every slot type:** `RANGED_VALUE` (percent 0 to 100, with the level as the arc), `SHORT_TEXT` ("47 %" with the trend icon), `LONG_TEXT` ("San Juan 46,8 % · 64,5 hm³"), and `SMALL_IMAGE`. They update hourly through `ComplicationDataSourceUpdateRequester` after a fetch.
3. **Round-first layout:** nothing in the corners, content inside the inscribed safe area, `TimeText` at the top, `ScreenScaffold` with an edge-hugging scroll indicator. Test on 192 dp (small) and 227 dp (large) round screens.
4. **The app mirrors the Apple Watch's three pages:** now (water and number), the 90-day curve with units and a capacity reference (F3), and the main figures with source and time. `TransformingLazyColumn` with rotary input.
5. **The same number style, labels and trend wording as the phone** (A5, F2, F4).
6. **Standalone but frugal:** fetch the Firestore REST snapshot directly when there is no phone. Use the Data Layer from the phone when paired, and WorkManager hourly with a network constraint. No Firebase SDK (CLAUDE.md).
7. **Ambient mode:** a low-bit outline of the water and the number, no motion, burn-in-safe shifting, and no gradients.
8. **Accessibility:** honour the Wear font-size setting at its maximum, TalkBack reads the summary sentence, touch targets of at least 48 dp, and never colour alone for the trend.
9. **Colour:** follow Wear dynamic colour for chrome, with the same fixed, harmonised water hue as the phone, legible on OLED black at every level (H8).
10. **Stale and offline states are designed,** with the full date shown on tile, complication and app, and **the formats are captured** (`wear-small`, `wear-large`, tile, each complication type) from day one (M1).

### iPad landscape

1. **The title, the water and the content share one margin.** The 1200 pt cap is a content margin, not a frame on the columns, and secondary screens use the same margin (N1).
2. **The water fills the window and Ahora is a full-width band of at least 40 % of the height,** with the number at 120 pt or more (D2, N5).
3. **The three sections sit side by side in landscape** (Ahora, Este año, El río), and two plus one in portrait (D3, D4, N5).
4. **Every capture scrolls,** so the bottom of the landscape dashboard is reviewed (N2, D5).
5. **Secondary screens use about a 680 pt readable width, or a two-pane layout,** with the history chart at 50 % or more of the height and the statistics beside it (D4).
6. **AX sizes stack label over value in the columns** and never truncate the source line (N4, D1, A1).
7. **Resizable windows are first-class:** layout follows size class from a third of the screen to full, the corner grabber covers no content, and windowed sizes are captured (N3).
8. **Keyboard and pointer:** ⌘R to refresh, ⌘[ to go back, hover effects on rows and links.
9. **Spanish region and storefront in captures:** "sáb 7 nov" and "0,99 €" (D7, A8).
10. **The same section placement and number style as the Android tablet,** so a user who owns both sees the same reservoir (P, H10, A5).
